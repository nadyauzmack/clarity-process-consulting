Remote-only consulting

Your processes. Your tools. Wherever your teams work.

Projects are completed virtually. I learn how the work happens through video meetings, shared screens, remote workflow walkthroughs, existing documentation, and approved system access when necessary.

The company doesn't have to be fully remote. The processes themselves must be reviewable and improvable remotely. I do not conduct on-site visits.
