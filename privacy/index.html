Privacy Notice

How we handle information you send through our inquiry form.

Information submitted

Our inquiry form asks for your name, email address, company name (optional), area of interest, and description of your project. Please do not send sensitive personal data or confidential client records through the form.

How we use it

We use your information to consider your inquiry and communicate about consulting services. Submitting the form does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

Providers

The site uses Cloudflare hosting, optional Cloudflare Turnstile spam protection, an email delivery service (Resend), and a Google hosted inbox for messages. These services may process the data necessary to deliver, receive, and protect inquiries under their applicable terms.

Retention and requests

We retain correspondence as needed for communication, business recordkeeping, and applicable obligations. Use our inquiry form to request information about a previous submission or request deletion where applicable.

Updates

This notice may be updated as our operations change. Last updated October 26, 2026.
