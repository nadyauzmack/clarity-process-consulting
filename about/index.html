The person behind Clarity

Hi, I'm Hope Uzmack.

I enjoy the work behind the work: noticing when a task is unnecessarily repeated, finding the gap between two teams, and turning confusing instructions into a clear system people can follow.

My background includes corporate Life New Business operations at a large national insurer, with progressive analyst responsibilities since 2022. I've worked inside regulated, high-volume processes where accuracy, documentation, training and cross-functional communication matter every day.

That environment taught me to look beyond surface-level “efficiency tips.” A solution needs to make sense to the employee doing the work, the colleague receiving it, the leaders tracking it and the controls that protect the business.

I founded Clarity Process Consulting to apply that perspective to remotely observable workflows in any industry where the work can be examined and redesigned digitally. I work independently and virtually; my consulting practice is not an extension of my corporate employer.

The Clarity Process Consulting approach
How I work

Observe. Question. Simplify. Document.

01

Understand

Ask employees to walk through the real process by video; identify what isn't captured in the written procedure.

02

Analyze

Differentiate necessary controls from repeated work, weak handoffs and unclear decisions.

03

Recommend or redesign

Deliver evidence-informed recommendations or build future-state workflows and SOPs, depending on the package.

04

Make it usable

Clarify ownership, training needs, exception routes and how teams can maintain the revised way of working.
