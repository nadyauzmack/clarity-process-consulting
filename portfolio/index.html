FRONTLINE JOB AID

Guest issue: quick reference guide

Learning goal: Know what to do when a routine customer issue needs attention.

  • Listen, clarify and confirm the concern.
  • Check the applicable policy or safety procedure.
  • Resolve within your authority or escalate.
  • Record relevant information and hand off as needed.

Example of a practical hospitality team workflow.

PROCESS DECISION GUIDE

Incomplete request: what happens next?

Trigger: An intake request reaches the team.

  • Required details complete? If no, request the missing information.
  • If complete, assign the next responsible owner.
  • Record the status and any outstanding exception.
  • Notify the customer or originating team of next steps.

Example decision tree that can be tailored to your team's workflow.

MICROLEARNING OUTLINE

Five minute learning module structure

Objective: Perform a new workflow correctly without relying on memory alone.

  • Explain the outcome and key rules.
  • Demonstrate a realistic scenario.
  • Practice one branching decision.
  • Check understanding with one question.
  • Provide a job aid for use after training.

Example storyboard for clear, structured employee training.

Selected professional experience · Workflow automation

From copy and paste follow ups to one click email drafts

In an insurance application workflow, staff tracked application status and outstanding requirements in an internal system. Preparing routine updates meant manually copying tracked details into a separate email and formatting the message repeatedly.

Improvement: A reusable template driven approach generates a structured email draft from tracked application and requirement information with a button click. The employee reviews the message before sending, reducing repetitive copying and helping keep communications consistent.

Where this applies: insurance underwriting and case management, healthcare authorizations, loan processing, credentialing, recruiting, and any team tracking outstanding documents or requirements.

Results: The workflow reduces manual assembly steps; the goal is faster, more consistent email preparation. Actual implementation depends on approved data access, existing software, and security controls.

An example of how I approach repetitive application updates and more efficient customer communications.
